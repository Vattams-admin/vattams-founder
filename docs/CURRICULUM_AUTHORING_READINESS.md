# Curriculum Authoring Readiness Gate

The controlled handoff between approved syllabus evidence/curriculum mapping and production authoring.

Authoring remains locked until an approved artifact is linked to the exact curriculum map and the Subject → Chapter → Topic → Subtopic hierarchy is complete.

Readiness never publishes content by itself. Quality review, provenance, answer-key privacy and assessment blueprint gates remain mandatory.

Official assessments keep correct answers and explanations private during attempts; practice/review can release them after submission according to policy.
